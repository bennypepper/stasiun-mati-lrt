SETTINGS
GAMEPLAY
DIFFICULTY How many infected spawn, how hard they hit, and how scarce ammo & health drops are.
TIME CYCLE NIGHT HORROR: dark blackout stations. DAYLIGHT–SUNSET: bright day-to-sunset, but infected are faster and spot you from farther.
DISPLAY
LIGHTING PRESET Cycles the sky & light mood in-game (also [L]). Does not change difficulty.
BRIGHTNESS Scales overall scene exposure. Raise it if dark areas are hard to read.
100% · DARK HORROR
GRAPHICS
QUALITY PRESET HIGH: sharper image, 1024 shadows, bloom (heaviest). BALANCED: 720p floor, soft shadows, no post-FX (best FPS). POTATO: no shadows, bloom or street clutter.
RENDER SCALE Internal resolution. Lower = more FPS but softer image; higher = sharper but costs GPU. [GPU: Low-High]
SHADOWS Sun shadows from props, pillars and infected. [GPU: Medium]
BLOOM Soft glow on lights and muzzle flashes. Enables a post-process pass. [GPU: Medium-High]
AMBIENT OCCLUSION Contact shadows in corners and under objects. HALF is cheaper than FULL. [GPU: High]
FPS COUNTER Shows frames per second in the corner (also [F3]).
AUDIO
SOUND Master mute for effects, music and Sarah's radio voice (also [M]).
